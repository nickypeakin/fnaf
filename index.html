How to play

Surviving the night

  • Survive from 12 AM until 6 AM. Freddy, Bonnie, Chica and Foxy roam the pizzeria and want in.
  • Doors (Q / E) block anyone in the doorway. Lights (A / D) show who is standing there.
  • Monitor (Space, or hover the bar at the bottom) shows the cameras. Click a camera on the map, or use the arrow keys.
  • You can't reach the door and light buttons while your monitor is up.
  • Everything uses power. If it runs out, the doors open, the lights die, and Freddy comes for you.
  • If something gets into the office, the door and light on that side stop working, and it strikes the moment anyone is off their monitor.

Playing together

  • Everyone is a guard in the same office. The doors, lights and power are shared, and every guard has their own monitor.
  • Split the jobs: someone on the left door, someone on the right, someone on the cameras.
  • Call out (F) flashes the camera you're watching, or the door you're facing, on everyone's screen.
  • The team list shows where everybody is looking. Dots on the camera map show which cameras your teammates are on.
  • Bigger crews face more aggressive animatronics, but get a longer-lasting battery.

Hosting

  • One person hosts and shares the 5-letter code (or the invite link). Everyone else joins with it.
  • Up to 6 guards per night; anyone else can watch.
  • No server needed. Players connect directly to the host's browser, so the host should keep the tab open.
