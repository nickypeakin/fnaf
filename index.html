How to play

Night guard

  • Survive from 12 AM until 6 AM.
  • Doors (Q / E) block anyone in the doorway. Lights (A / D) show who is standing there.
  • Monitor (Space, or hover the bar at the bottom) shows the cameras. Click a camera on the map, or use the arrow keys.
  • Everything uses power. If it runs out, the doors open, the lights die, and Freddy comes for you.
  • If someone gets into the office, the door and light on that side stop working. They'll get you as soon as you look away from the monitor.

Animatronics

  • Click a glowing room on the map to move there. Each move has a cooldown.
  • Yellow outlines on your map show which cameras the guards are watching. A big eye means they're looking at you.
  • Bonnie & Chica: get to your doorway, wait a few seconds, then Sneak in if the door is open. A door shut in your face sends you back.
  • Freddy: can only move while nobody is watching him. From the East Hall Corner he can slip in while the guard is on the monitor.
  • Foxy: creep out of Pirate Cove while nobody watches, then Run. A closed door costs the guard power.
  • Jam camera (J) fills your camera with static for a few seconds.

Multiplayer

  • One person hosts and shares the 5-letter code (or the invite link). Everyone else joins with it.
  • Up to 2 guards (co-op) and one player per animatronic. Animatronics nobody picks are run by the AI.
  • No server needed. Players connect directly to the host's browser, so the host should keep the tab open.
