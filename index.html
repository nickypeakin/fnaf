How to Play

Move W A S D · Jump Space · Sneak Shift · Sprint Ctrl or double-tap W

Break hold left mouse · Place / use right mouse · Pick block middle mouse

Inventory E · Drop Q · Hotbar 1–9 or scroll · Swap hands F

Chat / commands T or / · Debug F3 · Hide HUD F1 · Pause Esc

Fly (creative) double-tap Space · Zoom with a spyglass (hold right mouse)

Punch trees for logs → planks → crafting table. Open the recipe book (green book button) in any crafting grid to see every recipe and fill the grid with one click.

Commands: /gamemode creative|survival, /time set day|night, /tp x y z, /give item [count], /seed, /spawn, /kill, /weather clear
